About Me

BEng (Hons) Mechanical, Curtin University 2026 · MSc, University of Pretoria 2027 · Gauteng, South Africa

My work centres on simulating heat and fluid flow, and on checking that those simulations are actually right. My Honours thesis characterised the pore structure of eggplant mesocarp as an oil sorbent: a Python/OpenCV pipeline measured permeability from microscopy, and an independent ANSYS Fluent porous-zone model predicted the same value to within 0.17% — two methods, one answer, which is the only reason to believe either.

From 2027 I move to pore-scale Lattice Boltzmann simulation at the University of Pretoria’s CERG-FLUX Lab, modelling bio-inspired porous geometries for phase-change thermal energy storage under Prof. Muaaz Bhamjee. Alongside that I work across commercial and open-source solvers (ANSYS Fluent, OpenFOAM), scripted analysis in Python, and validation against published benchmarks.

Outside the solver: gearbox design to reliability specs, a solar-panel soiling rig built and instrumented from scratch, sustainable aviation fuel lifecycle modelling, and founding leadership of eight student engineering chapters. I am registering as an ECSA Candidate Engineer and looking for simulation and research roles in Gauteng.
